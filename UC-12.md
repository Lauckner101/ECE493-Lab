# UC-12: Register for Conference Attendance

## Use Case (Cockburn Style)

**Goal in Context**  
Allow an attendee to register for conference attendance and complete payment.

**Scope**  
Conference Management System (CMS)

**Level**  
User Goal

**Primary Actor**  
Attendee

**Secondary Actors**  
External Payment Service, Email Notification System, Database System

**Trigger**  
Attendee selects the **Conference Registration** option.

### Success End Condition
- Payment is successfully processed.
- The attendee is registered for the conference.
- A confirmation/ticket is generated and delivered.

### Failed End Condition
- Registration is not completed.
- No payment or ticket is recorded.

### Preconditions
- The attendee is registered and logged into the CMS.
- Conference registration is open.
- The CMS web application is available.

### Main Success Scenario
1. The attendee logs into the CMS.
2. The attendee navigates to conference registration.
3. The system displays attendance options and pricing.
4. The attendee selects an attendance type.
5. The system redirects the attendee to the payment interface.
6. The attendee enters valid payment information.
7. The attendee submits the payment.
8. The system processes the payment successfully.
9. The system records the registration.
10. The system generates a confirmation/ticket.
11. The system notifies the attendee of successful registration.

### Extensions
- **8a**: Payment is declined.  
  - The system displays a payment failure message and does not register the attendee.
- **6a**: Invalid payment information is entered.  
  - The system displays validation errors and requests correction.
- **9a**: A system error occurs while recording the registration.  
  - The system notifies the attendee that registration could not be completed.

## Fully Dressed Scenario Narratives

### Main Success Scenario Narrative
An attendee logs into the Conference Management System and navigates to the conference registration page. The system displays available attendance types and associated prices. The attendee selects an attendance type and is redirected to the payment interface. After entering valid payment information, the attendee submits the payment. The system processes the payment successfully, records the attendee’s registration, generates a confirmation ticket, and notifies the attendee of the successful registration.

### Alternative Scenario: Payment Declined
If the payment service declines the transaction, the system informs the attendee that payment was unsuccessful. The registration is not completed, and no ticket is generated. The attendee is prompted to retry or use a different payment method.

### Alternative Scenario: Invalid Payment Information
If the attendee enters invalid payment details, the system detects the error and displays validation messages. The attendee must correct the payment information before proceeding.

### Alternative Scenario: System Error During Registration
If a system error occurs after payment submission while recording the registration, the system notifies the attendee that the registration could not be completed. No ticket is issued, and the attendee may need to contact support or retry later.
