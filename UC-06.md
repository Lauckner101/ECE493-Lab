# UC-06: Assign Referees to Submitted Paper

## Use Case (Cockburn Style)

**Goal in Context**  
Allow an editor to assign referees to a submitted paper so that it can be reviewed.

**Scope**  
Conference Management System (CMS)

**Level**  
User Goal

**Primary Actor**  
Editor

**Secondary Actors**  
Referee (Reviewer), Email Notification System

**Trigger**  
Editor selects a submitted paper and chooses to assign referees.

### Success End Condition
- Exactly three referees are successfully assigned to the paper.
- Review invitations are sent to the assigned referees.

### Failed End Condition
- The paper does not have the required number of referees assigned.

### Preconditions
- The editor is registered and logged into the CMS.
- The paper has been successfully submitted.
- The CMS web application is available.

### Main Success Scenario
1. The editor selects a submitted paper.
2. The system displays the referee assignment interface.
3. The editor enters the email address of a referee.
4. The system checks the referee’s current workload.
5. The system assigns the referee to the paper.
6. The system sends a review invitation to the referee.
7. Steps 3–6 are repeated until three referees are assigned.
8. The system confirms that the paper has three assigned referees.

### Extensions
- **4a**: The referee already has five assigned papers.  
  - The system blocks the assignment and notifies the editor.
- **6a**: The referee is already assigned to the paper.  
  - The system displays a duplicate assignment error.
- **8a**: Fewer than three referees are assigned when the editor exits.  
  - The system marks the paper as incompletely assigned.

---

## Fully Dressed Scenario Narratives

### Main Success Scenario Narrative
An editor logs into the Conference Management System and selects a submitted paper that requires referee assignment. The system displays the assignment interface. The editor enters the email addresses of eligible referees. For each referee, the system verifies that the referee has fewer than five assigned papers, assigns the referee to the paper, and sends a review invitation. Once three referees have been assigned, the system confirms that the paper is ready for review.

### Alternative Scenario: Referee Workload Limit Exceeded
If the editor attempts to assign a referee who already has five assigned papers, the system detects the workload limit and blocks the assignment. The system informs the editor that the referee cannot be assigned additional papers.

### Alternative Scenario: Duplicate Referee Assignment
If the editor attempts to assign the same referee more than once to the same paper, the system detects the duplicate and prevents the assignment. The editor is prompted to choose a different referee.

### Alternative Scenario: Incomplete Referee Assignment
If the editor leaves the assignment process before three referees are assigned, the system marks the paper as partially assigned and indicates that additional referees are required.

## Acceptance Test Suite — UC-06

### AT-UC06-001 — Assign Three Referees Successfully
**Preconditions:** Editor logged in; eligible referees available.  
**Steps:**  
1. Select submitted paper  
2. Assign three eligible referees  

**Expected Results:**  
- Three referees assigned  
- Invitations sent  
- Paper marked as fully assigned  

---

### AT-UC06-002 — Referee Workload Limit Exceeded
**Preconditions:** Referee already has five assigned papers.  
**Steps:**  
1. Attempt to assign overloaded referee  

**Expected Results:**  
- Assignment blocked  
- Error message displayed  

---

### AT-UC06-003 — Duplicate Referee Assignment
**Preconditions:** Referee already assigned to paper.  
**Steps:**  
1. Attempt to assign same referee again  

**Expected Results:**  
- Duplicate assignment error  
- No duplicate record created  

---

### AT-UC06-004 — Fewer Than Three Referees Assigned
**Preconditions:** Paper has fewer than three referees assigned.  
**Steps:**  
1. Exit assignment interface  

**Expected Results:**  
- Paper marked as incompletely assigned  

---

### AT-UC06-005 — Referee Workload Increment
**Preconditions:** Referee eligible before assignment.  
**Steps:**  
1. Assign referee to paper  

**Expected Results:**  
- Referee workload count increases by one  

---

### AT-UC06-006 — Invitation Sent on Successful Assignment
**Preconditions:** Valid referee assignment.  
**Steps:**  
1. Assign referee  

**Expected Results:**  
- Review invitation sent  

---

### AT-UC06-007 — Prevent Over-Assignment Beyond Three Referees
**Preconditions:** Two referees already assigned.  
**Steps:**  
1. Attempt to assign two additional referees simultaneously  

**Expected Results:**  
- Only one additional referee assigned  
- Total referees does not exceed three
