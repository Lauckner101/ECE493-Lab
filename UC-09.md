# UC-09: Make Final Paper Decision

## Use Case (Cockburn Style)

**Goal in Context**  
Allow an editor to make the final accept or reject decision for a paper once reviews are complete.

**Scope**  
Conference Management System (CMS)

**Level**  
User Goal

**Primary Actor**  
Editor

**Secondary Actors**  
Author, Email Notification System

**Trigger**  
The paper has received the required number of completed reviews and is ready for decision.

### Success End Condition
- A final decision (accept or reject) is recorded for the paper.
- The author is notified of the decision.

### Failed End Condition
- No final decision is recorded.

### Preconditions
- The editor is registered and logged into the CMS.
- The paper has exactly three completed reviews.
- The CMS web application is available.

### Main Success Scenario
1. The editor logs into the CMS.
2. The editor navigates to the list of papers ready for decision.
3. The editor selects a paper.
4. The system displays the paper details and completed reviews.
5. The editor selects a final decision (Accept or Reject).
6. The editor submits the decision.
7. The system validates that all required reviews are present.
8. The system stores the final decision.
9. The system updates the paper’s status.
10. The system notifies the author of the decision.

### Extensions
- **7a**: Fewer than three reviews are completed.  
  - The system blocks decision submission and informs the editor.
- **5a**: No decision is selected.  
  - The system displays an error message requesting a decision.
- **8a**: A system error occurs while saving the decision.  
  - The system notifies the editor that the decision could not be saved.

## Fully Dressed Scenario Narratives

### Main Success Scenario Narrative
An editor logs into the Conference Management System and accesses the list of papers ready for final decision. The editor selects a paper and reviews the submitted referee evaluations. After considering the reviews, the editor selects either **Accept** or **Reject** and submits the decision. The system verifies that all required reviews are present, stores the decision, updates the paper’s status, and notifies the author of the outcome.

### Alternative Scenario: Insufficient Reviews
If the editor attempts to make a decision on a paper that has fewer than three completed reviews, the system blocks the action. The system informs the editor that additional reviews are required before a final decision can be made.

### Alternative Scenario: No Decision Selected
If the editor submits the decision form without selecting either **Accept** or **Reject**, the system detects the omission. The system displays an error message prompting the editor to select a valid decision.

### Alternative Scenario: System Error During Decision Save
If a system error occurs while saving the final decision, the system notifies the editor that the decision could not be recorded. The paper’s status remains unchanged, and the editor may retry after the issue is resolved.

## Acceptance Test Suite — UC-09

### AT-UC09-001 — Accept Paper Successfully
**Preconditions:** Paper has three completed reviews; editor logged in.  
**Steps:**  
1. Open paper ready for decision  
2. Select **Accept**  
3. Submit decision  

**Expected Results:**  
- Decision saved  
- Paper status set to Accepted  
- Author notified  

---

### AT-UC09-002 — Reject Paper Successfully
**Preconditions:** Paper has three completed reviews; editor logged in.  
**Steps:**  
1. Open paper ready for decision  
2. Select **Reject**  
3. Submit decision  

**Expected Results:**  
- Decision saved  
- Paper status set to Rejected  
- Author notified  

---

### AT-UC09-003 — Insufficient Reviews
**Preconditions:** Paper has fewer than three reviews.  
**Steps:**  
1. Attempt to submit decision  

**Expected Results:**  
- Submission blocked  
- Informational error displayed  

---

### AT-UC09-004 — No Decision Selected
**Preconditions:** Paper has three reviews.  
**Steps:**  
1. Submit decision form without selecting decision  

**Expected Results:**  
- Error message displayed  
- No decision saved  

---

### AT-UC09-005 — System Error While Saving Decision

**Steps:**

**Expected Results:**  
- Error message displayed  
- Decision not saved

- Correct decision communicated
